## Study 1 — October 13. Both stanzas in 14 pt Caslon.

Set the 1859 second stanza and the 1861 second stanza side by side. The 1861 is longer by about a line and a half; "Diadems – drop – and Doges – surrender –" runs to 31 picas and the measure was 30. Would have to go down to 12 pt or widen the measure. Widened to 34.

Dashes: the case has en and em dashes. Hers are neither. Used the en with thin spaces either side. Howe would hate it. Noted it for the colophon.

## Study 2 — October 15. The change, by hand, timed.

Took the 1859 stanza apart and set the 1861 in its place, not filming, just timing. 41 minutes. 212 sorts out, 247 in (counted from the script's output, see prompts/). The first stanza's one change ("Sleep" to "Lie") takes under a minute and I almost decided to leave it out of the film. Kept it: it's the one change a viewer can actually read.

## Study 3 — October 16. Framing test on the copy stand.

Top-down, phone on the stand, the chase filling the frame. 12 fps looks right for hands; 24 was too smooth and looked like a product video. Lighting from the left makes the type legible. Shot 30 seconds of test frames. The sound of the sorts going into the stick is good; the room's HVAC is not. Will record room tone separately and gate it.
