**A sample, not a real student's work.** Jo Lindqvist is invented, and so is everything here that isn't a public document. The poem, its two versions, Susan Dickinson's objection, and the books in `secondary/` are real. Written for TDM 155AI week 9 so the ensemble has something with several kinds of context to read: primary documents, secondary criticism, the student's own notes, process logs, AI output, and a few things that shouldn't be read at all.

## The piece

*Alabaster*: three letterpress broadsides and a 90-second stop-motion film. The broadsides set the two versions of Emily Dickinson's "Safe in their Alabaster Chambers" (1859 and 1861) and, on the third sheet, Susan Dickinson's one-line objection to the second verse. The film is the type for the second stanza being pulled apart and reset, sort by sort, from the 1859 version to the 1861 one. Finished October 30; the film is in `piece/` on the shared drive (not here).
