## Sharon Cameron, *Choosing Not Choosing: Dickinson's Fascicles* (1992)

The book about variants. Her argument, as I understand it: the variant words in the fascicles aren't drafts waiting for a decision, they're part of the poem; Dickinson keeps the alternatives open on purpose. "Choosing not choosing." I read the introduction and the chapter that deals with the fascicle context. This is the idea the piece is arguing with: a press can't not choose.

## Susan Howe, *My Emily Dickinson* (1985)

Less about this poem than about how editors flattened her. Howe is angry at the way the manuscripts were regularized. Useful for the colophon problem: I also regularized her, in metal. There's no dash sort that matches her marks.

## Cristanne Miller, ed., *Emily Dickinson's Poems: As She Preserved Them* (2016)

The edition I used for the texts alongside the Archive images. Prints the fascicle versions with the variants listed. Her note on Fr124 lays out the versions and the exchange with Sue.

## Marta Werner and Jen Bervin, *The Gorgeous Nothings* (2013)

The envelope poems, facsimile. Not this poem, but it's where I got the idea that the shape of the paper is part of the writing. The broadside format comes from looking at this.
