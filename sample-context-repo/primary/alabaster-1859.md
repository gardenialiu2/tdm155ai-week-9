---
title: "Safe in their Alabaster Chambers" — 1859 version (Fr124, J216)
source: my transcription from the Emily Dickinson Archive (edickinson.org) and Franklin's reading text; check dashes and capitals against the images before printing
---

Safe in their Alabaster Chambers –
Untouched by Morning –
And untouched by noon –
Sleep the meek members of the Resurrection –
Rafter of Satin – and Roof of Stone –

Light laughs the breeze
In her Castle above them –
Babbles the Bee in a stolid Ear,
Pipe the sweet Birds in ignorant cadence –
Ah, what sagacity perished here!

Note to self: Franklin dates this 1859. It's in Fascicle 10 (check). This is the version that went to the *Springfield Republican*.
