---
title: "Safe in their Alabaster Chambers" — 1861 version, the stanza sent to Sue
source: my transcription from the Emily Dickinson Archive; the first stanza is the same as 1859
---

Safe in their Alabaster Chambers –
Untouched by Morning –
And untouched by noon –
Lie the meek members of the Resurrection –
Rafter of Satin – and Roof of Stone –

Grand go the Years – in the Crescent – above them –
Worlds scoop their Arcs –
And Firmaments – row –
Diadems – drop – and Doges – surrender –
Soundless as dots – on a Disc of Snow –

Note: "Sleep" became "Lie" in the first stanza too, in some copies. I printed "Sleep" on sheet one and "Lie" on sheet two. Emily sent Sue at least one more alternative second stanza after this ("Springs – shake the Sills –"); I left it out of the piece. That's a choice I should be able to defend.
