title: Publication in the Springfield Republican, 1862
source: secondary accounts (Miller; the Dickinson Electronic Archives); I have not seen the newspaper page

The 1859 version was printed in the *Springfield Daily Republican* on March 1, 1862, under the title "The Sleeping," anonymously, with the newspaper's punctuation. Samuel Bowles edited the paper and was a friend of the family. It's one of the ten or so poems published in her lifetime, none under her name.

For the piece: I thought about a fourth sheet set the way the newspaper set it (title added, dashes turned into commas), as the "edited" version. Dropped it for time. Could be the thing to do next.
