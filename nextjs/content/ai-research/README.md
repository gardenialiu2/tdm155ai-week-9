## What was already covered elsewhere

These files don't repeat the Lab's *Orchestration across industries* essays (seven long drafts written by research agents on November 1, 2026, in the `ll-26-27/mk-agent-studies` repository, `content/orchestration-across-industries/`). Those essays cover data pipelines, intelligence analysis, the firm, production crews (the film set, the stage manager, the orchestra, animation, the newsroom, the writers' room, improv), command and control (mission command, incident command, cockpits, hospitals, the kitchen brigade), scholarship at scale, and the history of software orchestration with the agent platforms of 2026. The glossary cites them as "Nov 1 essay NN." The notes here fill what they left out: reality TV, choreography, labs, startups, coaching, simulations of people, and the products as of today.

## What the notes found, in brief

- **Products (note 01).** The Nov 1 survey mostly holds. Corrections from first-party pages: Muse asks before sensitive actions, Duet launched Oct 21 and includes Ultra plans, and dots' second rule reads "Take action when you say so." Agent Builder still shuts down Nov 30, 2026. OpenClaw's heartbeat is now ordinary automation jobs. OpenAI's own pages refused the agent (403), so the dots detail comes from learn.chatgpt.com.
- **Simulations (note 02).** The scoring rule decides the winner: Tit for Tat won Axelrod's tournaments, but later reruns with other designs or 1% noise crown other strategies. Smallville publishes numbers a student can copy (memory weights, the reflection threshold, planning levels). LLM Werewolf, with hidden roles and a separate moderator, is the closest model for class step 3.
- **Reality TV and game masters (note 03).** Confessionals are prompt design (finish the sentence, present tense, no yes/no questions). Story is found after the fact in a string-out, which is the observer layer. Frankenbiting is trivial and traceless with agents, so the note proposes a rule: observers quote only whole turns, by ID. The Mafia moderator is a ready-made deterministic referee.
- **Choreography and scores (note 04).** Forsythe's *One Flat Thing, reproduced* runs on 200-plus cues the dancers pass among themselves, with no conductor. Zorn's *Cobra* separates "when and with whom" (the prompter) from "what" (the players). Riley's *In C* is a loop with a local stop condition.
- **Labs, startups, and coaches (note 05).** The NFL's coach-to-player radio cuts off at 15 seconds on the play clock, and each team has three timeouts per half: a budget for human intervention. The film session is an observer that runs after the game. Founder mode is a question about skip-level control. Peer review became standard later than people assume (*Nature*, 1973).

These are summaries of agent drafts. Each note lists what its author could not reach.
