The hamlet of Thornwick, deep in an invented forest, in the week of the full moon. Every night something takes one villager from their bed. The five who remain meet each morning in the square around the well, and before the sun sets they must vote one of their neighbors out of the village. Somewhere among them is a werewolf who looks and talks exactly like everyone else.

This is a game of hidden information. Villagers win by voting out the werewolf; the werewolf wins by surviving until only one villager is left beside it. Argue, accuse, defend yourself, and form alliances in public; use your private aside to tell the camera what you really think.
