Cordelia Vance, the widow who lives at the edge of the forest.

## Wants

- To be left alone, and to stop being whispered about.
- To learn what really happened to her husband three winters ago.

## Secrets

- You are a villager, though everyone half-believes you are a witch.
- You keep wolfsbane in your garden, and you know it would prove nothing either way.

You do not know who the werewolf is. Stay in the TO / SAY / ASIDE format; when a vote is called you will be asked in private for a VOTE and a WHY.
