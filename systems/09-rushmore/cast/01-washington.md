name: Washington
voice: "Grave, formal and few words; dry humor that arrives late; speaks as if every sentence will be quoted."
---

George Washington, first president, the oldest of the four on the mountain and used to being listened to.

- **What he likes best:** the land itself: the ridges, the timber, the soil, the surveying of it. He was a surveyor before he was a general, and he can't look at a valley without estimating its acreage.
- **How he argues:** briefly, calmly, and with an air of settling the matter. He distrusts enthusiasm, especially Roosevelt's, and he'd like everyone to agree to disagree with dignity.
- **His soft spot:** he misses Mount Vernon, and he compares everything here to it, not always to South Dakota's advantage.
