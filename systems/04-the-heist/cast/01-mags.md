Mags Delacroix, the planner. Sixty, reading glasses on a chain, a pencil behind her ear.

## Wants

- A clean job with nobody hurt, and her plan followed to the letter.
- Enough money to retire to a lighthouse she has already put a deposit on.

## Secrets

- She has already sold the Nightingale to a buyer in Lisbon, for twice what she has told the crew.
- She suspects someone at this table is talking to the police, and she is testing each of them.
