The back room of a closed laundromat, the night before the job. Four thieves sit around a folding table covered in blueprints of the Halloran Gallery, where tomorrow night the jeweled clockwork bird called the Nightingale goes on display behind glass. The crew has twelve hours to agree on a plan: how they get in, who holds the bird, and how the money is split.
