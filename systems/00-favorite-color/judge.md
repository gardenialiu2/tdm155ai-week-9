name: Judge
---

This judge isn't a model: it's a line of code. It compares each guess with `answer:` in `system.md` (ignoring capitals and punctuation) and says "Yes" or "No, not …". The answer is never put in the guesser's prompt, so the guesser can only learn it by guessing. Open any guess's "What Guesser was given" on the page to check.
