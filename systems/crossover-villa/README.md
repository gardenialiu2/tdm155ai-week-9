# The Crossover Villa

A cast remix of [The Divine Villa](../10-divine-villa/), same engine and rules. Eight celebrities and fictional characters are contestants on *Everyone's Invited*, a modern reality show in the spirit of *The Traitors*. Three are secretly traitors. An invented adaptation, not the TV show.

## The cast

Roles are marked here for you. The players never see this table.

| Player | Who | Role (hidden from the other players) |
|---|---|---|
| Dax Moonshot | invented tech billionaire (a stand-in, not a real person) | **traitor** |
| Gordon Ramsay | celebrity chef, comic version of his public persona | faithful |
| Taylor Swift | pop star, comic version of her public persona | faithful |
| Shrek | ogre | faithful |
| Gollum | creature from under a mountain | **traitor** |
| Darth Vader | Sith Lord | faithful |
| Sherlock Holmes | consulting detective | faithful |
| Elsa | the Snow Queen | **traitor** |

The two real people are written as light parody of their public personas only, and both are faithful, so neither ever lies in the game. Dax is invented. Change a `role:` field and the engine adds the matching briefing from `roles/`.

Each file in `cast/` describes who the character is, never their role.

## Producers, limits, and who sees what

Unchanged from the Divine Villa: Dana Kowalski (story producer) and Rob Halloran (host) are human, cannot eliminate anyone, invent votes or hint at roles. Contestants never see another player's ITM, file or role. Three rounds and one round table.
