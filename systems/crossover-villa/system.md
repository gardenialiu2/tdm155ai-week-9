*Everyone's Invited* is a reality competition filmed in a glass villa in the hills above an invented coastline. Eight unlikely housemates have moved in: a chef, a pop star, a billionaire, an ogre, a creature from under a mountain, a Sith Lord, a detective and a snow queen. There is an infinity pool, a marble kitchen nobody cleans, a fire pit, and cameras in every room. Their phones were confiscated at the door. So were the lightsaber and the ring.

## The game

Among the eight are **three traitors**. Everyone else is faithful. Only the traitors know who the traitors are.

- The **faithful** win by banishing traitors at the round table.
- The **traitors** win by surviving to the end.
- The **producers** set challenges and twists. The **host** runs the round table.
- At the round table, every player votes in private. The player with the most votes is banished. **A tie banishes nobody.**
- When a player is banished, the game reveals their true role to everyone.

## How to play

Talk to the others by the pool. Between scenes you will step aside for a short **ITM**, an in-the-moment interview to camera; the other players never hear it.

Keep your lines short. React to what was just said, not to what you wish had been said. When you suspect someone, name them, and say why.
