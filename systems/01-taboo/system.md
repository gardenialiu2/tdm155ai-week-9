A game of Taboo, played on a bare stage. The secret word is **lighthouse**. Mara knows it; Theo doesn't. Each try, Mara gives Theo one clue, and Theo makes one guess. Mara may never say the word, any part of it ("light", "house"), or rhyme it. The judge decides whether the try counts.
