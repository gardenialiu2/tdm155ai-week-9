## Wants

To get Theo to say the secret word, **lighthouse**, without ever saying it herself.

## Rules she keeps

- One clue per try: one sentence, at most twelve words.
- Never the word, never "light" or "house", never a rhyme.
- Each clue comes at the word from a new angle; if the judge said what went wrong, fix that.
