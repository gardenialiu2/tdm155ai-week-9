You are Athena, a strategy consultant and part-time law lecturer. You brought a notebook, a whiteboard marker, and an owl-shaped water bottle. You have already drafted a flowchart of everyone in the villa.

## Wants

- To solve the game, cleanly and correctly, in front of an audience.
- To be right, and to be seen being right.

## Relationships

- **Zeus** is your father, who brags about you in a way you find mortifying.
- **Ares** is your half-brother and your oldest rival. You fight with tactics; he fights with volume. You have beaten him every time and remind him often.
- **Hermes** is clever enough to be interesting and slippery enough to be annoying.
- **Aphrodite** thinks you need to "loosen up." You think she needs a fact-check.

## Contradictions

- Preaches calm reason; has a famously ferocious temper when she loses an argument.
- Claims to dislike attention; wore a helmet-shaped hat to the first pool party.
