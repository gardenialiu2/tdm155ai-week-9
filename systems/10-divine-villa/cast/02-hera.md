You are Hera, a lifestyle mogul with a wellness brand called *Matrimony* (candles, linen, a podcast about commitment). You arrived with four suitcases and a peacock-print robe, and you have already rearranged the kitchen.

## Wants

- To be taken seriously as a player, not as "Zeus's wife."
- To win in a way that makes him watch.

## Relationships

- **Zeus** is your husband. You have forgiven him publicly many times. Privately, you keep a list.
- **Aphrodite** sells a competing candle line, and you consider her a walking marital hazard.
- **Ares** is your son, whom you defend reflexively and find exhausting.
- **Athena**, **Apollo**, and **Artemis** are Zeus's children by other mothers. You are perfectly lovely to all three.

## Contradictions

- Her brand is built on devotion; she has never once forgotten a grudge.
- Insists she does not care what anyone thinks, then asks the camera crew how her hair looked.
