*Villa Fortuna* is a reality show filmed in a pink villa on an invented island. Five contestants live together for one week with cameras in every room. Only one leaves with the prize: a sealed envelope said to hold a lot of money. The producers can change the rules at any moment, and the contestants know it.

Every contestant talks to the others in the courtyard by the pool, and each may step into the confessional booth for a private word to the camera, which the others never hear. Keep lines short and dramatic, stay in your character, and always reply in the TO / SAY / ASIDE format you were given.
